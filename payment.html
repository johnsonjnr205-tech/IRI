Terms & Conditions — Payment & Pricing

1. Pricing & Quotations. Prices vary by service type, scope, complexity, length, and deadline. Quotations are issued after reviewing your requirements and are valid for 7 days. Rush orders (delivery under 48–72 hours) attract a rush fee of 20–50% above the standard rate. Prices are quoted in Naira (₦) for local clients and in USD or equivalent for international clients.

2. Payment Structure. Standard orders require full payment upfront before work begins. Large/bulk orders (e.g. full theses, multi-chapter dissertations, major data projects) require a 50% deposit to commence, with the balance due before final delivery. Buy Now Pay Later options (Afterpay/Klarna) are subject to eligibility for qualifying international orders.

3. Order Confirmation. Orders are confirmed only after proof of payment is verified via WhatsApp or email. Work begins after confirmation; delays in sending proof may delay your delivery timeline.

4. Revisions & Refunds. Each service includes a set number of free revisions within a defined window after delivery, limited to the original brief. Revisions outside the original scope are quoted and billed separately. Full refunds are available only if work has not yet commenced; once underway, refunds are not available, though partial credit toward another service may be offered at our discretion. No refunds for completed work delivered as per the agreed brief.

5. Fees & Exchange Rates. Clients bear any transaction, conversion, gateway, or transfer fees charged by banks, processors, or crypto networks. Crypto payments are converted at the prevailing market rate at the time payment is confirmed.

6. Outstanding Balances. For installment/flexible plans, failure to settle the balance within the agreed timeframe may result in delivery being placed on hold, an admin/storage fee, or, after 30 days, forfeiture of the deposit.

7. Price Validity. Listed and quoted prices are subject to change without notice for new orders. A quote, once paid (in full or as a deposit), is locked in for that order.

8. Confidentiality. Payment details (account numbers, wallet addresses, payment links) shared with you are for that specific transaction only and should not be reused without confirmation from us.
